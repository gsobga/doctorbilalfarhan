# Bilal Call Updates: Visual and Content Refinement

## Goal
Bring the clarity and visual quality Bilal approved on the homepage to the rest of the site. Make care and education easier to navigate, reduce dense text, improve image consistency and motion, and correct the details discussed on the call.

## What will change

### 1. Clearer navigation and patient pathways
- Simplify the main navigation into clearly labeled, keyboard-accessible menus with visible active states and reliable mobile behavior.
- Standardize the practice name as **Center for Advanced Urology, a CLS Health practice** everywhere it appears.
- Rename the men’s-care label to clearly emphasize **Male Urinary Incontinence** while keeping BPH and HoLEP easy to find.
- Add consistent back, previous, next, condition, and procedure links so patients can move between related pages without returning to the homepage.
- Make every care option and procedure tile clickable, with each link opening the matching condition or patient guide.

### 2. Image-first care pages
- Rework BPH, male incontinence, women’s health, bladder/voiding, reconstruction, kidney-stone, and second-opinion pages to follow the homepage’s approved visual rhythm.
- Replace long text-heavy blocks with concise summaries, large approved images, and scannable treatment choices.
- Use the correct Bilal-approved image for each condition and procedure; keep image dimensions and presentation consistent without lowering source resolution.
- Remove mismatched or low-quality visuals, including the current GreenLight visual if no approved replacement exists.
- Add restrained image enlargement and reveal effects that remain usable with reduced motion.

### 3. Factual and wording corrections
- Change general contact center hours to **Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–5:00 PM; Saturday, 9:00 AM–1:00 PM; closed Sunday**.
- Remove the homepage claim **“Catheter-free for most patients within days.”**
- Keep BPH and HoLEP as a dedicated care area, with clear information and approved imagery for both.
- Correct mixed procedure/condition groupings: urethroplasty with urethral stricture, stone procedures with kidney stones, and continence procedures with the matching male or female condition.
- Remove the items Bilal rejected, including Peyronie’s disease and male sling complications from the care overview.
- Replace homepage references to “UTMB” in summary metrics with patient-friendly language such as **Seven years in academic urology** while retaining verified UTMB history where contextually appropriate.

### 4. Academic career, awards, and global surgery
- Correct the HoLEP program timeline to show it began in **February 2023**, passed 100 cases by February 2025, and now reflects **250+ procedures**.
- Replace the incorrect generic UTMB milestone destination with the best verified article or an on-site feature that keeps patients within Dr. Farhan’s current practice journey.
- Present academic photos in a consistent landscape or portrait system, prioritize high-resolution images, and remove awkwardly cropped or weak images.
- Turn awards and Centers of Excellence into visual, consistent features with direct links to relevant patient guides where appropriate.
- Enlarge global-surgery images and add accessible click-to-expand viewing with subtle motion.

### 5. Patient education hub
- Make Patient Education a simple three-part destination: **read about your condition**, **understand a procedure**, and **watch or download resources**.
- Add image-led, clickable procedure choices and reduce the number of steps needed to reach HoLEP, AUS, GreenLight, female sling, Botox, sacral neuromodulation, urethroplasty, and stone guides.
- Add print-friendly/downloadable patient handouts generated from the reviewed site content, clearly branded for Dr. Farhan and suitable for saving as PDF.
- Keep existing symptom questionnaires and preparation guidance easy to reach.

### 6. Motion, polish, and accessibility
- Replace the current busy aurora/orb effects with cleaner, restrained clinical motion: gentle reveals, image focus, small directional movement, and subtle background linework.
- Preserve the reduced-motion control and ensure all new motion respects it.
- Improve text sizing, spacing, contrast, focus states, menu controls, and tap targets for older patients and people with impaired vision.
- Keep the homepage composition Bilal rated “10 out of 10”; changes there will be limited to factual corrections, brand consistency, and navigation improvements.
